FastPlay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

FastPlay is a video player for Samsung Smart TV, Android and the web. It does not supply any content of its own: you sign in with an account from your own IPTV provider, and the application shows you what that provider offers.

This policy explains exactly what we store, why, and for how long. It applies to the FastPlay TV application, the mobile application and the web application.

What we collect

DataWhy
IP address and user agent Recorded on each sign-in attempt, to detect misuse of an access code — for example, one code being shared with many people.
Your provider username To keep your session open and to tie your access code to a single account.
Your provider's address The host name of the panel you sign in to. It scopes your favourites and your playback position, so that content from one provider is never mixed with another's.
Favourites and playback position So that the list you save and the point you stopped watching at are the same on your television, your phone and the web.
Your access code The code we issued to you, and the date it expires.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, e-mail address, telephone number or postal address.
  • Payment data. We never handle card details.
  • Your location, or any geographic data beyond what an IP address implies.
  • Any identifier of your television: no DUID, no serial number, no MAC address.
  • Camera, microphone, voice or biometric data. The application never requests them.
  • What you do outside FastPlay: no browsing history, no list of your other applications.
  • Your contacts, calendar, or files.

We show no advertising and we run no third-party analytics or tracking.

Your provider password

On the television, your provider password never reaches our servers. It travels from the television directly to your provider, and the television keeps it locally in order to play video.

On the web and mobile applications the password does reach our server, because there it is the server that fetches the catalogue from your provider on your behalf. When that happens it is stored encrypted, it is tied to your session, and it is deleted when the session expires. It is never shown, exported or shared.

Who else sees your data

Your IPTV provider. The application connects to the panel whose address you type in, so that provider sees your requests and your IP address. That provider is not ours, we do not control it and we do not choose it — you do. Its own privacy practices are its own.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else. There are no advertising networks, data brokers or analytics companies involved.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in records (IP and user agent): 30 days. A scheduled job deletes anything older, automatically.
  • Sessions: deleted when they expire or when you sign out.
  • Favourites and playback position: kept while your access code is valid, so that you find them where you left them. Deleted on request.

Children

FastPlay is not directed at children. The application includes a parental control that hides adult categories and asks for a numeric code before revealing them.

Your rights

You may ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to the address below and we will answer.

Deleting your data removes your favourites and your playback positions, and closes your sessions. Your provider account is not ours and is not affected.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
